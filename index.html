03 — ABOUT

Behind
The Lens

“I never went to film school. I just started.”

Four years ago, Wale was a graphic designer who got tired of static images, so he taught himself to make them move. No degree, no expensive gear, just a camera, a lot of bad first tries, and the stubborn belief that story matters more than credentials.

Today he's a Creative Director and Editor at a global organisation, CCI Global, and the founder of his own production company, ITAN Studios. He's shot short films, told stories for startups, and photographed events where you learn to think on your feet, or miss the shot.

His work spans four disciplines: design (posters, key art and brand identity), video (weddings, commercials and music), film (shorts, documentary and trailers) and photography (events and portraits).
